# An identity the install does not hold reaches nothing

## Why

The default customer's floor was granted to whatever identifier asked: resolving the level over the default customer for an id with no account answered read and write, and the list of reached customers always included the default. A session that outlived its account — the losing claimant's was one — went on reading and writing every unattributed case.

## What Changes

- The default customer is reached by holding an account; an identity with none reaches nothing.

## Impact

- `openspec/specs/accounts-and-access/spec.md`: *Case data is reached through groups, at a level* says so, and gains a scenario.
- The reach resolution consults the account before granting the floor, in both the level and the list of customers reached.
