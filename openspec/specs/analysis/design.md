# Scope

**Every view here is derived on read.** Nothing in this capability is stored, and no route publishes a view's own shape. What is stored is what the analyst recorded; the views are ways of reading it.

**Two published models are used and neither is ours.** One vocabulary describes what an attacker did; a second describes how far through an intrusion that puts them. One is derived from the other.

**The picture is of the intrusion.** What the analyst did to investigate is excluded, and so is drawing each recorded event as a thing in its own right.

**Narrowing to a stretch of time is a way of looking**, never a filter that changes the case.

**Search stays inside the case.** Finding a value across cases is a different question, and the API spec owns it.

# Design

## Where the attack got to is derived, never asked for

An analyst records what an attacker did in the vocabulary their industry already uses. Where that places the attacker in an intrusion is worked out from what they recorded.

**Asking for it separately would be double entry**, and double entry is how two fields come to disagree about one fact.

**The more specific reading wins.** A precise technique implies a stage more reliably than the general category it belongs to, so where both are recorded the precise one decides.

**The analyst can override, and the override wins over both.** The derivation is an inference and the analyst was there. There are also stages of an intrusion that no vocabulary of technique will ever imply — reaching the objective, and an insider breaching policy without any technique at all — and those are reachable only by saying so.

Because it is derived, it is correct the moment the recorded activity is corrected, and there is no second field to go stale.

## The picture is drawn from the case already on the wire

Nothing publishes nodes, links or positions. The picture is computed from what the screen already has, so it cannot disagree with the case, and there is no stored layout to migrate when the drawing changes.

**Events are not nodes.** Drawing every recorded event beside the things it happened to roughly doubles the picture and turns it into a diagram of the case file. An event is drawn as the movement between the things it names, which is what an analyst is looking for.

**The analyst's working-out is excluded.** How a case was investigated is not something the attacker touched. Mixing the two produces a picture of two activities at once, and neither reads.

The exclusion is by what is being pointed at rather than by the name of the field pointing, so a new way of referring to the analyst's working-out is excluded without anybody remembering to add it.

**A reference to something no longer there draws nothing**, rather than drawing a node for something the case does not hold.

**An analyst can take something out.** A case always holds something true and unilluminating, and the picture is a way of seeing rather than a record to be complete.

## Narrowing bounds itself to the case

The stretch an analyst may choose spans what the case actually covers rather than a calendar. An analyst is not asked to find their incident inside a year.

Nothing is removed and leaving the narrowing restores everything, because it is a view.
