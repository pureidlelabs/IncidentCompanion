# Compliance

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A determination the analyst records is the one the assessment carries

Where an analyst records the determination a regime turns on, the assessment MUST carry it, whatever the criteria derive. The application presents the criteria and the analyst makes the call; a derived verdict that overruled a recorded one would be the application asserting law, against the person who has to defend the reading.

The assessment MUST say that the verdict was recorded rather than derived, because a reader who cannot tell the two apart cannot tell whose reading they are looking at.

Whether the regime applies at all is a separate question and MUST NOT be settled this way. A recorded determination answers the test; it does not put an entity in scope, so one nobody has classified stays undecidable whatever is recorded against it.

#### Scenario: An analyst records the determination

- GIVEN a case whose criteria derive no verdict, or derive the opposite one
- WHEN the analyst records the determination
- THEN the assessment carries what they recorded
- AND it says the verdict was recorded rather than derived

#### Scenario: A recorded determination does not put an entity in scope

- GIVEN a case whose entity nobody has classified
- WHEN a determination is recorded against it
- THEN the assessment stays undecidable
- AND what is missing is still nameable
