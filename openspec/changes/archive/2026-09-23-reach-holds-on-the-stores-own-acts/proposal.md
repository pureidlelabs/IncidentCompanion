# Reach holds on the store's own acts

## Why

A non-author review of the store's reach decision found the places it still stopped short.

A live document stored itself as the one analyst whose edit it took last. When that analyst lost write before the store took the words, the store matched no row, which raises nothing, and the document was marked stored: every other analyst's words in it were dropped, with no log line. Two analysts writing one note, the second lowered to read inside the quiet moment, lost both sets of words.

The store's own move act checked only that the mover writes the case. Called directly, it refused a probe case where an unreached customer held its reference and moved the probe into that customer where it did not: the oracle the move rule closed at the route, one consumer away.

A write the store refused for reach reached the caller as a 500. The design record said it is answered as the case not being there.

Two acts that see past every caller's reach answered anybody: the case tally answered any caller, and the artefact census answered every digest beside the case holding it.

An administrator's view of somebody's reach still settled the level and the role's floor its own way, beside the store's.

A session whose account is gone was refused, and the refusal went unrecorded: the audit line named an account that no longer exists.

## What Changes

- **A live document is stored by a writer the store still lets write it.** It keeps everyone who wrote into it since it was last stored, and a store that takes it from none of them leaves it unsaved and says so, to be tried again.
- **The store's move act holds the move's rules itself**: the default customer is not a destination, and a case carrying a reference moves only to a customer the mover reaches, refused the same way whatever that customer holds.
- **A write the store refuses for reach is answered as the case not being there.**
- **The case tally answers only an administrator, and the census answers digests only.** Every act the application may call is classed by whom it answers.
- **An administrator is shown the store's level and floor**, and only the grant naming a level is worked out beside it.
- **A refused reach is recorded for a session whose account is gone**, naming who the session was issued to.

## Impact

- `live`, `accounts-and-access` and `the-api` specifications: one scenario added to one requirement in each.
- `state/design.md` and `cases/design.md`: who a live document is stored as, the store's own move, the role's floor as a store answer, and which acts answer whom.
- Every schema application creates the role's floor as its own store function.
