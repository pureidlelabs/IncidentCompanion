# Accepted words are kept

## Why

Prose an analyst typed while they could write was lost when their write was withdrawn, or their account disabled, before the words were stored. The save acted as a writer or as whoever asked, and the store refused anybody who no longer reached the record (#1228). Where reach is decided was the question, and Article III answered it only as an exception it did not have. The product never deletes an analyst's account, since offboarding is disabling, so a withdrawal is the case that loses words and a row removed by hand is its limit. Every announcement of a write also carried its writer, and no screen read it (#1229).

## What Changes

- **constitution**: Article III, 1.2.0. Reach is decided when a change is accepted, and keeping it is not a second decision. Whatever keeps an accepted change stores it into the record and the case it was accepted into, and nothing else.
- **live**: words accepted from an analyst who could write are stored and named for them, whatever they reach when the words are stored. A word sent after write is withdrawn is refused and never stored. Words land only in the record, and the case, they were addressed to.

## Impact

- Every save runs under one narrow identity, and its audit lines are written in the same act as the words.
- The application ends its connections before it stops listening, and waits for a save already under way.
- An install needs the identity provisioned. The application refuses to start without it.
- An announcement no longer carries its writer.
